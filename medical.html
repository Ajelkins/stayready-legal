Medical & Safety Disclaimer

Stay Ready by A&N Health Technologies · Beta · October 27, 2026

General wellness only

Stay Ready is designed for general health, fitness, activity, and recovery information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor, or prevent disease.

Recovery estimates are not medical facts

Recovery Scores, fatigue estimates, trends, activity-load information, and workout or fasting suggestions are approximations derived from available user input and device information. Data can be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, or missing. A high score does not guarantee wellness, alertness, or fitness for any task.

Not a fitness-for-duty decision

Do not use Stay Ready to decide whether you are safe to drive, operate machinery, work on a railroad, perform safety-sensitive duties, or disregard employer rules and legal requirements. Follow workplace safety standards, personal judgment, and professional guidance regardless of any app score.

Seek professional advice

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making important medical decisions or beginning or substantially changing exercise, fasting, nutrition, or recovery practices, especially if you have a health condition, symptoms, or take medication.

Emergencies

Stay Ready is not an emergency service. If you have a medical emergency or symptoms that may require immediate attention, call local emergency services.

Beta software

This is prerelease software under evaluation. Features, calculations, and availability can change, and beta results should not be relied upon for health or safety decisions.
