Privacy Policy

Stay Ready by A&N Health Technologies · Beta · Effective October 27, 2026

Who we are

Stay Ready is a health, fitness, and recovery application developed by A&N Health Technologies. Questions about privacy can be sent to ANHealthTechnologies@icloud.com.

Information you enter

Depending on which features you use, Stay Ready processes profile information and preferences, including age or date of birth, physical measurements, wellness goals, work schedules, equipment, workout preferences, nutrition choices, and entries you make about sleep, fatigue, soreness, work, fasting, workouts, and nutrition.

Apple Health information

With your permission, the iOS beta reads relevant data from Apple Health, including sleep sessions and stages, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), and steps. Some data may have been supplied to Apple Health by third-party wearables such as Garmin. You can change or revoke permission in iOS Health settings. Stay Ready does not need a wearable to provide its manual-entry features.

How information is used

Stay Ready uses your entries and permitted Apple Health data to calculate estimates of recovery and activity, present history, and provide general wellness and training guidance. Scores and suggestions are estimates and must not be treated as medical or occupational fitness determinations.

Storage and network services

The current beta stores application records in a SQLite database on your device and computes its recovery estimates locally. The application does not currently offer an A&N Health Technologies cloud account or health-record synchronization service. Apple, your wearable provider, and device backup or operating-system services may process information under their own policies. We are continuing to verify native SDK and backup behavior before external distribution.

Exports and sharing

If you select Export Diagnostics, Stay Ready generates a JSON file on your device and opens the device share sheet. That file can contain sensitive health, profile, work, activity, and diagnostic information. Nothing is sent to A&N Health Technologies through this export unless you choose to share it. Files shared with a recipient or another app are subject to the recipient’s handling and privacy practices. Only share diagnostics with someone you trust.

Advertising and sale of information

The current beta does not include a targeted advertising SDK, and A&N Health Technologies does not sell health information or use HealthKit information for advertising or marketing. Any future change in data practices will require a policy update and applicable user choices.

Retention, deletion, and permissions

Application records remain on the device until they are deleted through available app controls or the app is removed. Removing Stay Ready does not erase information in Apple Health, copies you exported or shared, or device backups. To erase Stay Ready's local histories, session records, baseline and learning data, and recovery diagnostics, select Delete My Data in the app's Data and Privacy settings after ending any active Work, Sleep, or Fasting session. This does not remove Apple Health data, previously exported files, or device backups. If Apple Health permission remains enabled, Stay Ready may read that information again. We cannot delete information already stored independently by Apple Health or third-party services.

Security

Stay Ready relies on device and operating-system protections for locally stored information. Do not share diagnostic exports without reviewing their contents. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

TestFlight and third-party platforms

During beta testing, Apple TestFlight may collect crash reports, feedback, and testing information subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Garmin or other wearable providers may independently process information before it reaches Apple Health. This policy describes Stay Ready’s own processing, not the operation of those separate services.

Children

Stay Ready is not directed to children younger than 13 and is not intended to provide medical guidance to minors.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as features or data handling change. A revised effective date will be shown here. Contact ANHealthTechnologies@icloud.com with privacy questions or requests.
